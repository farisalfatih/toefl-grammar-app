## 1. Subject-Verb Agreement

### Definisi
Kata kerja (verb) harus sesuai dengan subjek dalam hal **jumlah** (singular/plural). Ini adalah salah satu error paling sering diuji di TOEFL EPT.

### Kaidah Utama

**1.1 Singular subject → Singular verb**
Subjek tunggal menggunakan verb berakhiran **-s/-es** (present tense).

| ❌ Salah | ✅ Benar |
|----------|----------|
| The student *study* every night. | The student **studies** every night. |
| The committee *have* decided. | The committee **has** decided. |

---

**1.2 Plural subject → Plural verb**
Subjek jamak menggunakan verb tanpa -s/-es.

| ❌ Salah | ✅ Benar |
|----------|----------|
| The students *studies* every night. | The students **study** every night. |

---

**1.3 Frase Sisipan Tidak Mengubah Subjek**
Prepositional phrase, relative clause, atau appositive yang muncul di antara subjek dan verb **tidak mengubah agreement**.

```
The quality [of the products] has improved.
         ↑ frase sisipan — abaikan untuk menentukan verb
Subjek sesungguhnya = "quality" (singular) → has
```

> ❌ The quality of the products *have* improved.  
> ✅ The quality of the products **has** improved.

> ❌ The list of requirements *are* attached.  
> ✅ The list of requirements **is** attached.

---

**1.4 Indefinite Pronouns → Selalu Singular**

| Singular Indefinite Pronoun |
|-----------------------------|
| each, every, either, neither, one, anyone, everyone, someone, no one, nobody, everybody, somebody, anything, everything, nothing |

> ❌ Each of the students *are* required to attend.  
> ✅ Each of the students **is** required to attend.

> ❌ Everyone *have* submitted their work.  
> ✅ Everyone **has** submitted their work.

---

**1.5 Correlative Conjunctions (Either...or / Neither...nor)**
Verb mengikuti **subjek yang paling dekat**.

```
Neither the teacher  nor  the students  were  present.
                          ↑ subjek terdekat (plural) → were
```

> ❌ Either the manager or the employees *is* responsible.  
> ✅ Either the manager or the employees **are** responsible.

> ✅ Either the employees or the manager **is** responsible. *(manager terdekat)*

---

**1.6 "The number of" vs "A number of"**

| Ekspresi | Agreement | Contoh |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| **The number of** + plural noun | Singular | *The number of students* **is** increasing. |
| **A number of** + plural noun | Plural | *A number of students* **are** present. |

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Temukan subjek **sesungguhnya** terlebih dahulu — abaikan semua frase di antara koma, preposisi, atau relative clause. Cek: singular atau plural? Lalu pilih verb yang sesuai.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The number of students who fail the exam _____ increasing every year.  
a) are &nbsp;&nbsp; b) were &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **is** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) have been

> **Jawaban: c) is** — "The number of..." diperlakukan singular.

**2.** Neither the manager nor the employees _____ aware of the new policy.  
a) is &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **are** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) was &nbsp;&nbsp; d) were

> **Jawaban: b) are** — Verb mengikuti subjek terdekat "employees" (plural).

**3.** Every boy and girl _____ expected to participate in the event.  
a) are &nbsp;&nbsp; b) were &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **is** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) have

> **Jawaban: c) is** — "Every + noun + and + noun" diperlakukan singular.

## 2. Clause vs Phrase

### Definisi

| | Memiliki Subjek | Memiliki Verb | Dapat Berdiri Sendiri? |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Independent Clause** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Dependent Clause** | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| **Phrase** | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |

### Jenis-Jenis Clause

**2.1 Independent Clause**
Memiliki subjek + verb dan dapat berdiri sendiri sebagai kalimat utuh.

> ✅ *The scientist discovered a new element.* → Kalimat lengkap

---

**2.2 Dependent Clause**
Memiliki subjek + verb tetapi **tidak dapat berdiri sendiri**. Dimulai dengan:
- Subordinating conjunction: *although, because, since, when, while, if, unless, after, before, until*
- Relative pronoun: *who, which, that, whose, whom*
- Noun clause marker: *that, whether, what, how*

> ❌ *Although it was raining.* → Fragmen  
> ✅ *Although it was raining,* **they continued the match.** → Lengkap

---

**2.3 Jenis-Jenis Phrase**

| Jenis Phrase | Contoh | Fungsi |
|---|---|---|
| Noun Phrase | *the tall building* | Subjek/Objek |
| Prepositional Phrase | *in the morning* | Adverb/Adjective |
| Participial Phrase | *running fast* | Adjective |
| Infinitive Phrase | *to study hard* | Noun/Adjective/Adverb |
| Gerund Phrase | *studying every night* | Noun |

---

**2.4 Aturan Kunci: Satu Kalimat = Minimal Satu Independent Clause**

```
❌ Because she studied hard. She passed the exam.   ← dua fragmen
✅ Because she studied hard, she passed the exam.   ← benar
✅ She studied hard; therefore, she passed.         ← benar
```

**Menggabungkan dua independent clause:**

| Cara | Contoh |
|------|--------|
| + FANBOYS (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) | She studied hard, **so** she passed. |
| + Semicolon | She studied hard**;** she passed. |
| Subordinating conjunction | **Because** she studied hard, she passed. |

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Identifikasi setiap Subject + Verb dalam kalimat. Pastikan ada minimal 1 independent clause. Waspada terhadap dependent clause yang "terpisah" dengan tanda titik — itu adalah fragmen.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** Which of the following is a CLAUSE (bukan phrase)?  
a) running in the park &nbsp;&nbsp; b) a beautiful sunset &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **when the rain stopped** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) the old wooden bridge

> **Jawaban: c)** — Punya subjek (the rain) + verb (stopped).

**2.** _____ the population of the city grew rapidly, new infrastructure was needed.  
a) Despite &nbsp;&nbsp; b) Because of &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **As** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) In spite of

> **Jawaban: c) As** — Subordinating conjunction yang memperkenalkan dependent clause. Opsi lain memperkenalkan phrase.

## 3. Subjunctive

### Definisi
Subjunctive digunakan untuk mengekspresikan **permintaan, saran, keharusan,** atau **situasi hipotetis**. Ciri khasnya: menggunakan **bentuk dasar verb (base form)** tanpa perubahan apapun.

### Formulanya

```
[Trigger Verb/Adjective] + that + Subject + V (base form)
```

### Kaidah Utama

**3.1 Trigger Verbs → Subjunctive**

| Kategori | Verb |
|---|---|
| Permintaan | suggest, recommend, request, ask, demand, require, insist |
| Perintah | order, command, urge, propose |
| Saran | advise, prefer |

> ❌ The doctor recommended that he *takes* the medicine.  
> ✅ The doctor recommended that he **take** the medicine.

> ❌ She insisted that he *attends* the meeting.  
> ✅ She insisted that he **attend** the meeting.

---

**3.2 Trigger Adjectives → Subjunctive**

| Adjective |
|---|
| essential, necessary, important, crucial, imperative, vital, advisable, desirable |

> ❌ It is essential that every student *is* present.  
> ✅ It is essential that every student **be** present.

> ❌ It is crucial that the data *was* verified.  
> ✅ It is crucial that the data **be** verified.

---

**3.3 Negative Subjunctive**
Gunakan **not + base form** (bukan don't/doesn't/didn't).

> ❌ The committee insisted that he *doesn't attend* the meeting.  
> ✅ The committee insisted that he **not attend** the meeting.

---

**3.4 Passive Subjunctive**
Base form of "be" + past participle.

> ✅ The manager requested that the report **be submitted** immediately.  
> ✅ It is necessary that all applications **be reviewed** by the committee.

---

**3.5 Hypothetical Subjunctive: "Were"**
Gunakan **were** untuk semua subjek (termasuk I/he/she/it) dalam kondisi hipotetis atau contrary-to-fact.

> ❌ If I *was* you, I would study harder.  
> ✅ If I **were** you, I would study harder.

> ✅ I wish he **were** here right now.  
> ✅ She acts as if she **were** the boss.

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Setelah trigger word + **THAT** → selalu base form verb, tidak peduli subjeknya I/he/she/they. "Be" tidak berubah menjadi is/are/was — tetap "be".

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The professor insisted that the student _____ the paper before the deadline.  
a) submits &nbsp;&nbsp; b) submitted &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **submit** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) would submit

> **Jawaban: c) submit** — "Insisted that" → subjunctive → base form.

**2.** It is vital that every participant _____ the safety guidelines.  
a) follows &nbsp;&nbsp; b) followed &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **follow** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) will follow

> **Jawaban: c) follow** — "It is vital that" → subjunctive → base form.

**3.** The manager requested that the report _____ to all department heads.  
a) is distributed &nbsp;&nbsp; b) are distributed &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **be distributed** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) will be distributed

> **Jawaban: c) be distributed** — Subjunctive passive → "be + V3".

## 4. Conditional

### Definisi
Kalimat kondisional mengekspresikan hubungan sebab-akibat berdasarkan tingkat kemungkinan atau realitas suatu kondisi.

### Empat Tipe Conditional

**4.1 Type 0 — General Truth / Scientific Fact**

```
If + Present Simple  →  Present Simple
```

Digunakan untuk fakta umum atau kebenaran ilmiah.

> ✅ If you **heat** water to 100°C, it **boils**.  
> ✅ If plants don't **get** sunlight, they **die**.

---

**4.2 Type 1 — Real/Possible Future**

```
If + Present Simple  →  will/can/may + V1
```

Kemungkinan nyata di masa depan.

> ❌ If it *will rain*, we will cancel the event.  
> ✅ If it **rains**, we **will cancel** the event.

> ✅ If you **study** hard, you **can pass** the exam.

---

**4.3 Type 2 — Unreal Present / Hypothetical**

```
If + Past Simple (were for all subjects)  →  would/could/might + V1
```

Situasi yang tidak nyata atau tidak mungkin di masa kini.

> ❌ If I *was* a doctor, I would help more people.  
> ✅ If I **were** a doctor, I **would help** more people.

> ✅ If she **knew** the answer, she **would tell** us.

---

**4.4 Type 3 — Unreal Past / Regret**

```
If + Past Perfect (had + V3)  →  would/could/might + have + V3
```

Kondisi yang berlawanan dengan fakta di masa lalu (penyesalan).

> ❌ If she *studied* harder, she would have passed.  
> ✅ If she **had studied** harder, she **would have passed**.

> ✅ If he **had taken** the medicine, he **would have recovered** faster.

---

**4.5 Mixed Conditional**
Kondisi masa lalu — hasil masa kini.

```
If + Past Perfect  →  would + V1 (now)
```

> ✅ If he **had taken** the job offer, he **would be** rich now.  
> ✅ If she **had studied** medicine, she **would be** a doctor today.

---

### Ringkasan Tabel

| Tipe | If-Clause | Main Clause | Contoh Situasi |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| 0 | Present Simple | Present Simple | Fakta ilmiah |
| 1 | Present Simple | will + V1 | Rencana masa depan |
| 2 | Past Simple / were | would + V1 | Impian/hipotetis |
| 3 | Past Perfect | would have + V3 | Penyesalan masa lalu |
| Mixed | Past Perfect | would + V1 | Efek masa lalu di masa kini |

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Perhatikan konsistensi tense antara if-clause dan main clause. TOEFL sering menjebak dengan mencampur tipe, misalnya: "If she *studied* harder, she *would have passed*" — ini salah (tipe 2 dicampur hasil tipe 3).

### Latihan Soal

**1.** If the researchers _____ more funding, they could have completed the project.  
a) received &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **had received** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) have received &nbsp;&nbsp; d) would receive

> **Jawaban: b) had received** — "Could have completed" → Type 3 → if-clause harus Past Perfect.

**2.** If water _____ below 0°C, it freezes.  
a) **cools** &nbsp;&nbsp; b) cooled &nbsp;&nbsp; c) would cool &nbsp;&nbsp; d) had cooled

> **Jawaban: a) cools** — Fakta ilmiah → Type 0 → Present Simple.

**3.** She would be healthier now if she _____ a better lifestyle in her youth.  
a) lives &nbsp;&nbsp; b) lived &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **had lived** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) has lived

> **Jawaban: c) had lived** — "Would be now" (present result) + kondisi masa lalu → Mixed Conditional.

## 5. Inversion

### Definisi
Inversi terjadi ketika **auxiliary verb mendahului subjek**. Digunakan untuk penekanan atau wajib dipakai setelah ekspresi negatif/restrictive di awal kalimat.

### Formula

```
Negative/Restrictive Adverb + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb
```

### Kaidah Utama

**5.1 Negative/Restrictive Adverb di Awal Kalimat**

| Ekspresi Pemicu | Contoh Inversi |
|---|---|
| Never | **Never have** I seen such beauty. |
| Rarely / Seldom | **Seldom does** she complain. |
| Hardly / Scarcely | **Hardly had** they arrived when it rained. |
| Little | **Little did** he know the truth. |
| Not only | **Not only did** she win, but she also broke the record. |
| Not until | **Not until** he apologized **did** she forgive him. |
| Only then | **Only then did** they realize the mistake. |
| Only after | **Only after** the meeting **did** he leave. |
| Only when | **Only when** she called **did** he respond. |
| No sooner | **No sooner had** she left **than** it started raining. |

---

**5.2 No sooner...than / Hardly...when**

```
No sooner + had + Subject + V3  →  than + Subject + V (past simple)
Hardly/Scarcely + had + Subject + V3  →  when + Subject + V (past simple)
```

> ❌ No sooner *she had left* than it started raining.  
> ✅ **No sooner had she left** than it started raining.

> ✅ **Hardly had the meeting started** when the fire alarm went off.

---

**5.3 So/Such + Adjective di Awal**

```
So + Adjective + Auxiliary + Subject + ...
```

> ❌ So difficult the exam was that many students failed.  
> ✅ **So difficult was** the exam that many students failed.

> ✅ **So impressive were** the results that the committee applauded.

---

**5.4 Only + Phrase/Clause di Awal**

> ❌ Only after studying for hours he understood.  
> ✅ **Only after studying for hours did he** understand.

> ✅ **Only by working together can we** solve this problem.

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Cari adverb negatif/restrictive di awal kalimat → itu sinyal inversi wajib. Pastikan auxiliary (do/does/did/have/has/had/is/are/was/were/will/would) mendahului subjek.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** Not until she arrived at the airport _____ she realized she had forgotten her passport.  
a) **did** &nbsp;&nbsp; b) had &nbsp;&nbsp; c) was &nbsp;&nbsp; d) does

> **Jawaban: a) did** — "Not until + clause" → inversi dengan "did" (Past Simple).

**2.** Seldom _____ such dedication in young researchers.  
a) we have seen &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **have we seen** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) we had seen &nbsp;&nbsp; d) had we saw

> **Jawaban: b) have we seen** — "Seldom" → inversi → auxiliary mendahului subjek.

**3.** _____ had the team finished the report when the client requested changes.  
a) Rarely &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **No sooner** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) Hardly &nbsp;&nbsp; d) Only

> **Jawaban: b) No sooner** — "No sooner had... when/than..." adalah structure yang baku.

## 6. Parallel Structure

### Definisi
Elemen-elemen yang setara dalam kalimat **harus menggunakan bentuk gramatikal yang sama**. Sering diuji bersama koordinator dan konjungsi korelatif.

### Kaidah Utama

**6.1 Koordinator: and, but, or, nor, yet**

Elemen yang dihubungkan harus dalam bentuk yang sama.

| ❌ Salah | ✅ Benar |
|---|---|
| She likes *reading, to write, and painted*. | She likes **reading, writing, and painting**. |
| He is smart, *works hard*, and honest. | He is **smart, hardworking, and honest**. |
| The report was *lengthy, confusing, and had no conclusion*. | The report was **lengthy, confusing, and inconclusive**. |

---

**6.2 Konjungsi Korelatif**

| Konjungsi | Formula Paralel |
|---|---|
| Both...and | Both [X] and [X] |
| Either...or | Either [X] or [X] |
| Neither...nor | Neither [X] nor [X] |
| Not only...but also | Not only [X] but also [X] |
| Whether...or | Whether [X] or [X] |

> ❌ He is not only *intelligent* but also *works hard*.  
> ✅ He is not only **intelligent** but also **hardworking**.

> ❌ She is known for both *her intelligence* and *working efficiently*.  
> ✅ She is known for both **her intelligence** and **her efficiency**.

---

**6.3 Paralel dalam Seri Infinitive**
Jika dimulai dengan infinitive, lanjutkan dengan infinitive (to bisa dihilangkan setelah yang pertama).

> ❌ She wants *to travel, studying abroad, and to learn* new languages.  
> ✅ She wants **to travel, (to) study abroad, and (to) learn** new languages.

---

**6.4 Paralel dalam Comparisons**

> ❌ *Swimming* is better exercise than *to walk*.  
> ✅ **Swimming** is better exercise than **walking**.

> ❌ *To err* is human; *forgiving* is divine.  
> ✅ **To err** is human; **to forgive** is divine.

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Temukan coordinator atau konjungsi korelatif → cek apakah semua elemen yang dihubungkan punya bentuk gramatikal yang sama (noun-noun, V-ing-V-ing, adjective-adjective, dsb).

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The new policy requires employees to attend training, _____ their schedules, and report any issues.  
a) updating &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **to update** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) updated &nbsp;&nbsp; d) update

> **Jawaban: b) to update** — Paralel dengan "to attend" dan "to report".

**2.** She is known not only for her intelligence but also for _____.  
a) she works hard &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **her hard work** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) working hard &nbsp;&nbsp; d) the way she works

> **Jawaban: b) her hard work** — "Not only for + noun phrase" → "but also for + noun phrase".

## 7. Relative Clause

### Definisi
Relative clause adalah dependent clause yang memodifikasi noun, menggunakan **relative pronoun** (who, whom, whose, which, that) atau **relative adverb** (where, when, why).

### Pemilihan Relative Pronoun

| Pronoun | Digunakan untuk | Fungsi dalam klausa |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | orang | subjek |
| **whom** | orang | objek |
| **whose** | orang/benda | possessive |
| **which** | benda/hewan | subjek atau objek |
| **that** | orang/benda (defining) | subjek atau objek |
| **where** | tempat | menggantikan there |
| **when** | waktu | menggantikan then |
| **why** | alasan (setelah "reason") | menggantikan for that reason |

### Kaidah Utama

**7.1 Who vs Whom**

```
Who   = menggantikan SUBJEK    (= he/she/they)
Whom  = menggantikan OBJEK     (= him/her/them)
```

> ✅ The scientist **who** discovered the cure received a Nobel Prize.  
> *(He discovered the cure → who)*

> ✅ The person **whom** I met yesterday is a professor.  
> *(I met him → whom)*

**Trik:** Ganti dengan he/him. Jika "he" cocok → **who**. Jika "him" cocok → **whom**.

---

**7.2 Defining vs Non-Defining Clause**

| | Defining Clause | Non-Defining Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Koma | ✗ tidak ada koma | ✓ ada koma |
| Pronoun | that/which/who | which/who (BUKAN that) |
| Fungsi | mempersempit makna noun | menambah informasi |
| Jika dihilangkan | makna berubah | makna tetap |

> ✅ The book **that** I borrowed is interesting. *(defining — buku yang mana?)*  
> ✅ This book, **which** was published in 1990, is still relevant. *(non-defining)*  
> ❌ This book, **that** was published in 1990, ... *(that tidak bisa untuk non-defining)*

---

**7.3 Whose**
Menggantikan possessive. Setelah "whose" langsung diikuti noun.

> ❌ The student *which project* won the competition is from our class.  
> ✅ The student **whose project** won the competition is from our class.

---

**7.4 Avoid Double Pronoun**
Jangan letakkan pronoun ganda setelah relative pronoun.

> ❌ The city where I was born **there** is now a metropolis.  
> ✅ The city **where** I was born is now a metropolis.

> ❌ The woman who **she** helped me is my aunt.  
> ✅ The woman **who** helped me is my aunt.

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Cek: (1) apakah ada koma? → non-defining → pakai which/who, bukan that. (2) Apakah pronoun menggantikan subjek atau objek? (3) Pastikan tidak ada double pronoun.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The researcher _____ work has been widely cited will give a lecture tomorrow.  
a) who &nbsp;&nbsp; b) whom &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **whose** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) which

> **Jawaban: c) whose** — "work" adalah noun yang membutuhkan possessive → "whose work".

**2.** The experiment, _____ took three years to complete, produced surprising results.  
a) that &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **which** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) who &nbsp;&nbsp; d) what

> **Jawaban: b) which** — Ada koma → non-defining clause → harus "which", bukan "that".

**3.** She is the one _____ I think deserves the award.  
a) **who** &nbsp;&nbsp; b) whom &nbsp;&nbsp; c) whose &nbsp;&nbsp; d) which

> **Jawaban: a) who** — "I think" adalah parenthetical insertion. Sesungguhnya: "She is the one who deserves..." → subjek → who.

## 8. Gerund vs Infinitive

### Definisi
- **Gerund** = V + -ing (berfungsi sebagai noun)
- **Infinitive** = to + V1

Pilihan antara keduanya bergantung pada **verb atau preposition** yang mendahuluinya.

### Daftar Verb

**8.1 Verbs + Gerund (V-ing)**

| Verb |
|---|
| enjoy, avoid, finish, consider, suggest, recommend, practice, mind, deny, admit, miss, keep, delay, postpone, resist, risk, quit, discuss, imagine, involve |

> ❌ She *enjoys to read* scientific journals.  
> ✅ She **enjoys reading** scientific journals.

> ❌ He *finished to write* the report.  
> ✅ He **finished writing** the report.

---

**8.2 Verbs + Infinitive (to + V1)**

| Verb |
|---|
| want, wish, hope, decide, plan, agree, refuse, promise, seem, appear, tend, manage, fail, afford, expect, need, offer, desire, intend, prepare, choose, learn |

> ❌ He *decided pursuing* a master's degree.  
> ✅ He **decided to pursue** a master's degree.

> ❌ She *promised helping* him with the project.  
> ✅ She **promised to help** him with the project.

---

**8.3 Verbs + Both (Makna Berbeda!)**

| Verb | + Gerund | + Infinitive |
|---|---|---|
| **stop** | stop *talking* = berhenti berbicara | stop *to talk* = berhenti (lalu) berbicara |
| **remember** | remember *locking* = ingat (sudah mengunci) | remember *to lock* = ingat (untuk mengunci) |
| **forget** | forget *sending* = lupa (sudah mengirim) | forget *to send* = lupa (untuk mengirim) |
| **try** | try *studying* = mencoba (metode) belajar | try *to study* = berusaha belajar |
| **regret** | regret *saying* = menyesal (sudah berkata) | regret *to say* = menyesal (harus mengatakan) |
| **go on** | go on *talking* = terus berbicara | go on *to talk* = kemudian berbicara |

> ✅ I **remember locking** the door. *(sudah dilakukan)*  
> ✅ Remember **to lock** the door. *(belum dilakukan, pengingat)*

---

**8.4 Preposition + Gerund**
Setelah preposition **selalu** gunakan gerund.

> ❌ She is interested *in to study* abroad.  
> ✅ She is interested **in studying** abroad.

> ✅ He is good **at solving** problems.  
> ✅ She left **without saying** goodbye.  
> ✅ They succeeded **in completing** the task.

---

**8.5 Gerund sebagai Subjek**

> ✅ **Running every morning** is healthy.  
> ✅ **Learning a new language** takes time.

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Hafal daftar verb + gerund dan verb + infinitive. Untuk yang bermakna ganda (stop, remember, forget, try, regret), perhatikan konteks: apakah aksi sudah terjadi (gerund) atau belum/akan terjadi (infinitive)?

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The committee decided _____ the project due to budget constraints.  
a) postponing &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **to postpone** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) postpone &nbsp;&nbsp; d) postponed

> **Jawaban: b) to postpone** — "Decided" → verb + infinitive.

**2.** I remember _____ the keys on the table, but now they are gone.  
a) to put &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **putting** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) put &nbsp;&nbsp; d) to putting

> **Jawaban: b) putting** — "Remember + gerund" = ingat tentang sesuatu yang sudah dilakukan.

**3.** Scientists are working on _____ a cure for the disease.  
a) to develop &nbsp;&nbsp; b) develop &nbsp;&nbsp; c) **developing** &nbsp;&nbsp; d) developed

> **Jawaban: c) developing** — "Working on" → preposition "on" → harus diikuti gerund.

---

## 9. Reduced Clause

### Definisi
Reduced clause adalah penyederhanaan dari **relative clause** atau **adverbial clause** dengan menghilangkan relative pronoun dan/atau auxiliary verb untuk efisiensi.

### Formulanya

```
Active:  who/that + is/are/was/were  →  V-ing
Passive: which/that + is/are/was/were + V3  →  V3 (past participle)
```

### Kaidah Utama

**9.1 Reduced Relative Clause — Active**
Hilangkan relative pronoun + be, atau ubah verb menjadi V-ing.

| Kalimat Penuh | Reduced |
|---|---|
| The woman **who is standing** near the door is my professor. | The woman **standing** near the door is my professor. |
| The students **who were taking** notes learned more. | The students **taking** notes learned more. |

---

**9.2 Reduced Relative Clause — Passive**
Hilangkan relative pronoun + be, tinggal V3.

| Kalimat Penuh | Reduced |
|---|---|
| The report **that was submitted** yesterday had errors. | The report **submitted** yesterday had errors. |
| The building **that was designed** by Zaha Hadid is iconic. | The building **designed** by Zaha Hadid is iconic. |

---

**9.3 Reduced Adverbial Clause**
Jika subjek sama dengan main clause, adverbial clause dapat direduksi.

| Kalimat Penuh | Reduced |
|---|---|
| **After she finished** the experiment, she wrote the report. | **After finishing** the experiment, she wrote the report. |
| **Because he felt** sick, he went to the doctor. | **Feeling** sick, he went to the doctor. |
| **After she had eaten** dinner, she studied. | **Having eaten** dinner, she studied. |

```
Aksi bersamaan    : V-ing
Aksi sebelumnya   : Having + V3
Aksi pasif        : V3 / Having been + V3
```

---

**9.4 Dangling Modifier — ERROR Klasik TOEFL**
Participial phrase harus memiliki subjek yang sama dengan main clause.

> ❌ *Running late,* **the bus was missed.** ← Siapa yang terlambat? Bus?  
> ✅ *Running late,* **she missed the bus.** ← "she" yang terlambat ✓

> ❌ *Exhausted after the marathon,* **a long nap was needed.**  
> ✅ *Exhausted after the marathon,* **she needed a long nap.**

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Untuk menghindari dangling modifier: pastikan subjek tersirat dari participial phrase **sama dengan** subjek kalimat utama. Jika berbeda → ubah salah satunya.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** The documents _____ by the committee were later destroyed.  
a) reviewing &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **reviewed** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) that reviewed &nbsp;&nbsp; d) to review

> **Jawaban: b) reviewed** — Reduced dari "that were reviewed by the committee" → passive → past participle.

**2.** _____ a major earthquake, the city required massive reconstruction.  
a) **Devastated by** &nbsp;&nbsp; b) Having devastated by &nbsp;&nbsp; c) Being devastated &nbsp;&nbsp; d) To be devastated by

> **Jawaban: a) Devastated by** — Reduced dari "Because it was devastated by..." → passive reduced clause.

**3.** _____ the data carefully, the researchers discovered an unexpected pattern.  
a) Analyzed &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **Analyzing** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) To analyze &nbsp;&nbsp; d) Having analyzed by

> **Jawaban: b) Analyzing** — Reduced dari "While they were analyzing..." → active V-ing.

## 10. Modifier

### Definisi
Modifier adalah kata, frase, atau klausa yang memberikan informasi tambahan tentang kata lain. **Modifier harus ditempatkan sedekat mungkin dengan kata yang dimodifikasinya.**

### Jenis-Jenis Error Modifier

**10.1 Misplaced Modifier**
Modifier yang terletak jauh dari kata yang dimodifikasi → makna ambigu atau salah.

> ❌ She *almost* drove her children to school every day. *(hampir mengantar?)*  
> ✅ She drove her children to school *almost* **every day**. *(hampir setiap hari)*

> ❌ I *only* told John about the secret. *(hanya memberitahu John? atau baru memberitahu?)*  
> ✅ I told *only* **John** about the secret. *(hanya John, bukan orang lain)*

**Kata yang sering salah penempatan:** *only, just, even, almost, nearly, hardly, merely, simply*

---

**10.2 Squinting Modifier**
Modifier yang dapat merujuk ke dua arah (sebelum atau sesudah).

> ❌ Students who study **often** get good grades. *(sering belajar atau sering dapat nilai bagus?)*  
> ✅ Students who **often study** get good grades.  
> ✅ Students who study get good grades **often**.

---

**10.3 Dangling Participle**
Participial phrase yang subjeknya tidak sesuai dengan main clause.

> ❌ *Running late,* **the bus was missed.** *(bus yang terlambat?)*  
> ✅ *Running late,* **she missed the bus.**

> ❌ *After finishing the experiment,* **the results were published.** *(siapa yang menyelesaikan?)*  
> ✅ *After finishing the experiment,* **the researchers published the results.**

---

**10.4 Adjective vs Adverb**

| | Memodifikasi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Adjective** | Noun / Pronoun | She is a **fluent** speaker. |
| **Adverb** | Verb / Adjective / Adverb | She speaks **fluently**. |

> ❌ She speaks *fluent*. *(fluent = adjective, tidak bisa memodifikasi verb)*  
> ✅ She speaks **fluently**. *(fluently = adverb)*

> ❌ The patient recovered *surprising* fast.  
> ✅ The patient recovered **surprisingly** fast. *(surprisingly memodifikasi adjective "fast")*

---

**10.5 Modifier Klausa vs Frase**

| Modifier | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Adjective Clause | The book **that I borrowed** is interesting. |
| Adjective Phrase | The book **on the table** is mine. |
| Adverb Clause | **Although it rained,** the event continued. |
| Adverb Phrase | **Despite the rain,** the event continued. |

---

### 💡 Tips TOEFL
> Tempatkan modifier **sesegera mungkin** setelah kata yang dimodifikasi. Waspada dengan participial phrase di awal kalimat — subjeknya harus sama dengan subjek main clause.

### Latihan Soal

**1.** Find the error: "The professor explained the theory to the students that was confusing."  
a) No error  
b) "that" should be "which"  
c) **"that was confusing" should modify "theory", bukan "students"**  
d) "explained" should be "had explained"

> **Jawaban: c)** — Misplaced modifier. Seharusnya: "the confusing theory" atau "the theory that was confusing to the students".

**2.** _____ a long time, the solution was finally found by the researchers.  
a) Searching for &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **Having searched for** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) After searched for &nbsp;&nbsp; d) Searched for

> **Jawaban: b) Having searched for** — Menunjukkan aksi yang terjadi sebelum main clause; subjek tersirat "the researchers" cocok dengan main clause.

**3.** The announcement stated that the event would begin _____.  
a) at 8 o'clock sharp only &nbsp;&nbsp; b) **only at 8 o'clock sharp** &nbsp;&nbsp; c) at only 8 sharp o'clock &nbsp;&nbsp; d) sharp only at 8 o'clock

> **Jawaban: b) only at 8 o'clock sharp** — "Only" memodifikasi "at 8 o'clock sharp", sehingga harus ditempatkan langsung sebelumnya.
